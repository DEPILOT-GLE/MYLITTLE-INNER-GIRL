a letter, written slowly

Mummie

There is no rush and nothing you have to answer. Open it whenever your heart feels ready.

What I have learnt about you

I have learnt that you love deeply, and I have never doubted it. As far as I know, you truly love me, and I trust you for sure.

But I have also noticed that there comes a time when everything starts to feel like pressure, like too much to handle. Even when nothing bad has happened between us.

When things become intimate, something in you gets alarmed. The closeness that should feel like rest begins to feel like a warning. And I think in that moment you wonder: is something broken? Is he really the right person? Are we moving too fast? Did I hurry into this? Has his love changed?

Then you step back. Sometimes you ask for silence. Sometimes you bury yourself in something else. Never because you want to hurt me, but because it feels like the safer decision.

You will always justify walking away, not because you want to, but because you believe it is the better choice. You are guarding your peace, and I see that.

As love grows deeper, I think a quiet story begins to form: that love should not feel this overwhelming. In that story, my closeness starts to look like neediness. I understand why the story is there. It makes leaving feel logical instead of painful. It softens the guilt. It lets the mind win over the heart.

Because love that asks for honesty, effort and presence can feel frightening. So calm, effortless love begins to look like the right kind. But calm that comes from distance is only protection. It is not always peace.

And I believe you are protecting a tender place in you, one shaped by what you have lived through, and by what you have watched other people go through.

Some hearts do not run from love. They run from the fear that love will ask them to be fully seen.

The part I never understood

For a long time I kept asking myself how you would give me love the same way I give it. And maybe that was where I went wrong.

I have come to realize that the way most people feel love is not always the way I want it to be felt. You love in your own language, quieter, slower, sometimes from further away. That does not make it less. It is simply who you are, and I am learning to listen.

To me you are not a problem to be solved. You are a girl with a different personality, one that needs to be understood.

I also know how heavy it is on your side. You have said you think you are full of chaos, that you bring pain to the people you love. I do not see chaos. I see someone who is afraid of feeling too much and afraid of hurting anyone.

And honestly, it hurts sometimes to be quiet with someone you love, care about and are used to. But I would rather tell you that softly than let you carry it as guilt.

How hard you are on yourself

Sometimes you go hard on yourself. When someone shows you love, kindness or energy, you feel you must give back exactly the same, and quickly.

So you start loading yourself with commitments that feel like debts to repay. Not because you truly want them, but because part of you believes people will not understand who you are beyond the beautiful, angelic eyes, the cute smile, the innocent girl they think they know.

Mummie, you do not have to earn kindness by exhausting yourself. Love is not a debt. You can receive it, rest inside it, and give back in your own time and in your own way.

And I have seen the other side of you too, and I love her just as much. The stubborn little princess who gets upset when things do not go her way, who kicks her legs when the blanket is wrapped around her, who is clingy and dramatic and completely real.

Nobody has to be only the angel. I do not need you to be. The girl who smiles and the girl who sulks are the same girl, and both of them are welcome here.

About your space

When you ask for space, I stand at a crossroads. Should I give it to you completely, or stay close and check in? Because sometimes the way it reaches me is like you are saying: I want you back.

So here is what I promise. I will give you room, and I will keep a light on. Not a spotlight. Just a light, so you can always see where home is.

And there is one thing I want to say gently. The day you wondered whether I wanted you back because of intimacy, it stung, mummie. Your beauty is precious to me, but I have never come to you only to satisfy myself. It was always you: your heart, your mind, the person you become when you feel safe.

Peace is not the absence of closeness. It is the feeling of being safe inside it.

A few gentle questions

These are only for you. Keep the answers to yourself, or share them when it feels right. There is no correct answer and no deadline. Tap one when you feel like sitting with it.

When closeness starts to feel heavy, where do you feel it first? In your chest, in your thoughts, in the urge to disappear for a while?

Only for you. No answer needed.

When you go quiet, what do you think I am thinking? And what do you think I am feeling? I would love to know how close that is to the truth, only if you want to share.

Only for you. No answer needed.

If closeness did not have to be a promise or a pressure, what would a safe version of it look like for you?

Only for you. No answer needed.

What would it feel like to be loved and not have to prove anything, not to be perfect, not to be strong, not to explain yourself?

Only for you. No answer needed.

Is there something in this letter that is not true for you? Which part did I get wrong? I would honestly like to understand you better.

Only for you. No answer needed.

Today, do you want me near, a little further away, or somewhere in between? And can that answer be allowed to change, without anyone being hurt by it?

Only for you. No answer needed.

Pause here with me

Let your shoulders drop. Nothing needs solving in this minute.

Something to carry gently

All the things you overthink will slowly drain you, my love. You do not have to hold everything, control everything, or solve every ending before it arrives. Loosen your grip a little, and let God take over.

What is meant for you will not need to be forced, and what is heavy does not have to be carried alone.

I thank God for helping me realize something: sometimes it is not that something is always wrong. Sometimes you just need someone to understand you.

Not everything heavy is broken. Some things are simply deep.

Look at what you have already lived

Life has not only been hard for you. There have been good days, warm meals, real laughter, and moments you were proud of. Tap the ones that feel like yours, and let yourself enjoy them again.

Your laugh is one of the best sounds I know. Keep making room for it.

Good food and no rush. Life hands us small feasts, and you deserve every one.

You have had those days before. It means you can have them again.

Hold on to that one. You earned it.

You have been carried by kindness more than you remember.

Peace is not a stranger to you. You have lived it, and it knows your name.

Is there a good memory of your own?

It stays on this screen only. Nobody else will see it.

The part I want you to keep

I am willing to walk with you.

I will always care, even in the moments when it is not returned, until you fully find your ease with yourself and with love. I am proud of your achievements, the ones I can see and the ones I never will. As long as I am here, you will never feel alone.

It is my desire to be a proud man who did not give up on you. Not out of pressure, and not to keep score. Out of love, and out of patience.

You do not have to answer tonight. You do not have to be different tomorrow. You only have to know this: you are seen, you are understood, and you are loved.

Always here for you.

Take your time. Come back to this whenever you need it.
